Getting Started

Taking the first step can be simple.

You don't have to know exactly what you need before reaching out. We can start there.

1

Reach out

Text 912-621-2810 for brief administrative questions or scheduling. Texting is preferred.

2

Choose an appointment

Use the secure Carepatron booking page to view available appointments.

3

Complete intake paperwork

Complete required intake, consent, privacy, and practice forms before beginning therapy.

4

Begin your first session

Talk about what brings you in, what you hope will change, and how therapy can best support you.

5

Build your plan together

Treatment is collaborative. Goals and interventions can evolve as your needs change.

Communication policy

Brief administrative communication may occur by text. Clinical discussion and sensitive information should take place during scheduled sessions or through the secure patient portal. Phone calls are used when clinically or administratively necessary.

Texts and portal messages are not monitored continuously and should never be used for emergencies.
