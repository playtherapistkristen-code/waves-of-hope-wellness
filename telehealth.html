Telehealth

Therapy that fits your real life.

Telehealth is an important option for clients who want quality therapy without adding another trip to an already busy day.

LESS TRAVEL. MORE FLEXIBILITY.

Skip the commute.

For busy parents, professionals, caregivers, and families, getting to therapy can sometimes be the hardest part of starting therapy.

Telehealth allows eligible clients to attend from an appropriate private space at home or another suitable location—without adding another drive, waiting room, or interruption to an already full day.

  • No commute to the office
  • Convenient around work schedules
  • Helpful for busy parents and caregivers
  • Less time away from home or business
  • Comfort of an appropriate private space
  • Greater flexibility for ongoing care
